A ~30-second walkthrough of AI Support Desk, for recording a screen-capture GIF/video or for a live walkthrough in an interview. Follow it against the live deployment: **https://ai-support-desk-gilt.vercel.app**

## Talking points while recording

- "Every ticket is triaged by a real LLM call — `gpt-4o-mini` with OpenAI's native structured-output enforcement, double-validated with Zod on the way back."
- "The ticket is saved _before_ the AI call runs, so a customer's message is never lost even if the AI call times out or fails — it just gets flagged `needs_review` instead."
- "Same Zod schema validates the form on the client for instant feedback, and again on the server, which is the actual source of truth."
- "Security headers, a locked-down CSP, and rate limiting are on every response — see `next.config.ts` and `src/lib/api/rate-limit.ts`."

## Recording tips

- Keep the browser window at a standard desktop width (1280–1440px) so the two-column ticket detail layout is visible.
- If OpenAI credits are available at recording time, submit two tickets — one clearly urgent ("production is down for all customers") and one clearly low-priority ("small typo on the pricing page") — to show the priority classification actually differentiating.
- Tools like [ScreenToGif](https://www.screentogif.com/) (Windows) or `ffmpeg`/QuickTime (macOS) work well for a 15–30s capture; keep the final GIF under ~5MB for a README embed.
